About the PayIntel crawler

PayIntel observes the publicly visible payment infrastructure of online stores: which payment providers and payment methods a checkout offers. The crawler reads public pages, adds a product to a cart and walks to the payment step. It never places an order, never submits a payment and never enters real card data.
